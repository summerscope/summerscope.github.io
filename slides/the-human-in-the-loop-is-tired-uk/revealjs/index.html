Slop or not? (A)


import time
def retry(fn, attempts=3, base_delay=1.0):
    for attempt in range(attempts):
        try:
            return fn()
        except Exception:
            if attempt == attempts - 1:
                raise
            time.sleep(base_delay * 2 ** attempt)
Not slop — looks right, is right.

Why it's not steerable

  • slow
  • noisy
  • missing feedback

Remedies & balms

what actually helps

optimise for human experience, not model output

The turn the section hangs on.

If you can't pop the hood, at least measure the exhaust

observability / tracing

Tracing = closest thing to a developer view today (OTel / Logfire); in-room callback — "as Tun and Fei covered in Friday's tutorial" (MCP tracing with OpenTelemetry).

If you're going to gamble, learn to count your cards

evals

Know the game, know what can go wrong; evaluation is the skill, make the tacit explicit; Aimee's chocolate-chip-cookie workshop; pays off Slop-or-Not; in-room callback — "as Samuel mentioned in the keynote" (codemode, Logfire as the verification step).

Technical balms

  • pre-mortems
  • AGENTS.md from your review comments
  • permission to just write the code
Pre-mortem = fresh session "assume it failed, why?"; distil your judgement; mode-switching (pick two).

Make the output brain-friendly

respect your cognitive limit (~4–7 chunks)

Aimee — chunk it; ask for a "scientific-abstract" not an "executive summary"; bold first/last sentences to speed-read.
