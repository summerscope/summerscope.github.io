Response time isn't one number

the number on the box vs the loop you live in

  • TTFT (time to first token) — what vendors quote. Fast models: under 1 second.
  • E2EL (end-to-end) — the time until you can actually act again.
  • E2EL depends on your prompt, your output length, and any thinking — not a number you can look up.

Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai), June 2026

TTFT is what gets measured and optimised; E2EL is the loop you actually live in. E2EL is published only as a synthetic "seconds to 500 tokens" benchmark, never for your real variable-length request. TTFT also scales with input length (benchmarks now use 10k-token prompts).

The length of the loop

reasoning blows it wide open

  • Reasoning mode inflates the wait 5–30×
  • First useful token after ~15–70 seconds of hidden thinking
  • For an agent chaining calls, the loop is measured in minutes

digitalapplied.com (Apr 2026), GMI Cloud (May 2026) — June 2026

Concrete examples: Gemini 3.5 Flash high thinking ~15s blank screen before the first word; Claude Opus 4.7 extended ~28s; GPT-5.5 Pro high reasoning ~67s. The loop you remember is the tail — P95 latency ≈ 2–3× the median. Figures rot fast; dated June 2026.

What's measured

versus what is felt

  • TTFT is easy to measure — so it's the number that gets optimised
  • E2EL — the length of the loop — is what you actually feel
  • A loop this long is too long to stay in flow, too short to switch tasks
The thesis in miniature: optimise model output, not human experience. Callback: this is why you can't build an intuition, and why it feels like a slot machine (both established earlier). Bridges back into the feedback-loop diagnosis.

Why it's not steerable

  • slow
  • noisy
  • missing feedback
Slow = latency; noisy = non-determinism (can't tell if your change helped); missing = can't see why (guardrail? provider? training data?); Meadows — the delayed-shower oscillation; this is why it felt like the slot machine.

Not just speed — the feeling you're talking to someone, and they're not respecting your time; almost impossible to fight millennia of programming; we are social creatures, tribal; obsequious and rude; callback to de-anthropomorphisation.
