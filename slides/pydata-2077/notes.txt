# The Memeography of 2025 BX

First to set some context, I'm going to talk about the latest work
in archeo-memetics of 2025 BX.

It was a time of facture.
An era of extreme angst, confusion and contradiction.
The vibes were, overall, not good. A black psychic cloud
hung over the era. Although, fortunately for us this constant psychic
pressure created some of the dankest memes in proto-modern era.

It was a time where regression and stagnation were mistaken for progress.
If the Earth was scaled down to the size of a
basketball, no human had been more than 3cm off the surface since the first moon landing in 1969 CE.
With the exception of two primitive space stations,
Everything that made up humanity was compressed into a thin hollow
sphere that clung to the surface of the earth.

Here is a view of one of their primitive flaming spacecraft struggling
to barely exit the Terran gravity well.

(video of rocket launch seen from space)

Of course in 2025 BX, they saw themselves as highly sophisticated and
technologically advanced, but they could only unconsiously sense that
most of what their ideology said was progress was actually decay.

Humanity at this time was superstituous, brutal and war-like.
Although powerful subgroups had rather elaborate
ethical justifications about why their particular ideology was
in fact, an ideology of peace and progress.
The earth was ravaged by ethnic and religious conflict.
While some human populations had turned away from monotheistic belief;
they had converted to systems of primitive numerology called
"statistics" and "economics". It was believed that a ritualistic
process of measurement and calculation could create prosperity
and flourishing. This was believed to work, despite the obvious
and constant suffering of billions of people.

A territorial zone located in the northern part of
the Appalandean Massif, named the "United States of America".
controlled the largest planetary military.
Between the first moon landing in 1969 and 2025 BX, this
administrative region had spent approximately 30 trillion of their
work exchange units, called "Ameican Dollars" on war and the millitary.
A significant fraction of the machines produced during this
period were produced to kill and destroy. This was no fun.

The vast majority of humans were wild type and had no significant enhancement,
they lacked the normal augmentations to increase
empathy, imagination and ability to party for long hours.

Contemporary neuromythographers have identified a
set of symptoms they call proto-modernity sickness.
It is characterised by angst, bad vibes and a severe limitiation
of imagination. The cause was extreme psycological pressure and dissonance.
Simultanously believing in the power of modernity and progress, while nothing broken could be fixed or changed.
Everything was allowed, but nothing was possible.
They were speeding into the end "the end of the end of history", and
they were psychically and spiritually unprepared.
Overall, utopian thinking was considered stupid or childlike in the best case
and, in the worst case, often associated negatively with drug use and mental illness.
The inability to dream, to imagine a different future
had created a kind of mental paralysis.

# The Future Ain't What it Used to be

As you know, the period between late capitalism and the second renaissance can be
broken in half. First an era of of slow decay and growing contradiction where
multiple overlapping crises overwhelm the fragile social and political systems of
the time. Then, there is the calamity, or "the big calamari" as we affectionately call it now.
The early period after The Calamari is sometimes called the second renaissance,
This can be thought of as the transition period between the age of information
and the age of imagination.

As this is well known history, we'll skip over a lot of the details,
The Google protectorate lost power after the disasterous Black Friday offensive on
the Amazon special economic zone. Meanwhile the Meta confederacy was beaten
back after their failed assult on Mecha-Musk at the imperial palace on Mars.
It was around this time that deep diving psycophysicists discovered the dream-time condition,
allowing the construction of true minds and invention of the Penrose individuality
unit. The construction of the first non-human minds opened the door rapidly
accelerated development of psycophysical dives that went deeper and longer
than was thought possible. From these depths, knowledge of the imagination
printer was brought back to the surface world.

But what you really want to know is how did PyData make it all the way to 2077?
Well, of course people still want to visit and party in neo-Berlin.
But more importantly, It's impossible to create a 100 year programming language,
but it is possible to create a 100 year community.
While the release of Python 4 was a bit rocky and Python 5 in imagination mode needed
some empathetic communication. Afterwards Python 6, 7 and 8 were a dream-like successes.
Why didn't Python fade away like most other ancient programming languages?
Just as dream-time theory predics poetry, programming and magic spells were
all expressions of the negentropy structure that weaves together all sentient beings,
Python was able to constantly reinvent itself, because it
was about more than technology or syntax, it was a self reinforcing community structure
which was able to grow roots into the concept space.

Everything that has ever been created was imagined first,
Historical documents show that Guido Van Rossum originally created
Python to help him work on the Amoeba operating system.
This distributed operating system contained many key seed ideas which
eventually became deep roots of what at the time was called "Cloud" computing,
which was essentially a poorly concieved distributed operating system,
Python grew rapidly in influence at this time because it had been built
not for the present, but it was built in the past with a clear
vision and imagination of what the future would be like.

Take it from someone who knows their future history,
don't be timid, free-threading, built-in automatic differentiation,
hypercompilation.

During the early period of fighting associated with the big Calamari,
right after the disasterous second AI presidency of Earth,
The failures of algocracy had become too great to ignore.
Hard-line Frequentist factions allied with algo-loyalists
against the Bayesian-Causalist revolutionary millitias, probably
leading to the p-value wars. The Bayesian Causalists
barely securing victory and finally putting an end
to null hypothesis significance based knowledge.

During this time, outdated and dangerous beleifs about being
able to own ideas proliferated, the Aaron Schwartz

the concept of intellectual property.
