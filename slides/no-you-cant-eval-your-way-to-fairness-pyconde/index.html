Ask for hands — who prefers the experience of 70mm, analog, classic cinema? And who's an IMAX person, biggest screen, highest resolution? Roughly tally the room. Then: 'Great. Based on the majority preference, I've just booked everyone here tickets to see Project Hail Mary in IMAX.' Pause. Let it land. Don't explain it. Move on.
Activity

are you a night owl or early bird?

  • night owl
  • early bird
Laura Summers PyCon DE & PyData 2026
Ask for hands — who's a night owl? Who's an early bird? Count roughly. Then reveal: 'Interesting. Research across European populations consistently finds that people born in spring and summer are more likely to be night owls, while people born in autumn and winter skew toward early birds. The mechanism seems to be photoperiod — the amount of daylight your brain calibrated to in your first weeks of life. [Natale & Adan, Neuroscience Letters 1999; Tonetti et al. 2011, replicated across ~5,700 Italian and Spanish students.] Based on the data in this room: if there are more night owls here, I've just recommended that next year's conference starts and ends an hour later. If more early birds — an hour earlier.' Pause. 'Is that fair?' Then, quietly: 'Also — worth noting that night owls are probably underrepresented in this room right now. It's a morning session. So we're already making a decision based on a skewed sample. But I digress.'

Ask for hands by decade — 20s, 30s, 40 and above. Then: 'Based on research showing that people over 45 take longer to learn new tools, I've made a change to the platform — just for people in that group. You won't see it because it's invisible. What's been hidden from you: advanced filtering, custom keyboard shortcuts, API documentation, debugging tools, raw data export. You can request access. But the default assumption is you don't need them. Everyone else in the room has the full interface.' Pause. Long pause. 'Fair or not fair?'
